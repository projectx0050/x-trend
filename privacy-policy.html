01

Who We Are and What This Covers

X-Trend is an AI writing service operated by Haris Mumtaz, a sole trader, ABN 14 400 350 871 ("we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use the X-Trend Chrome extension, our website, your X-Trend account and the X-Trend Telegram bot.

We have written this policy with the Australian Privacy Principles in mind, and it explains how to contact us or complain if you are concerned about your privacy.

02

Information We Collect

Your account

  • Your email address and whether you have verified it
  • Your password, stored only as a secure one-way hash, so we cannot read it
  • The date your account was created

Signing in and account security

  • Sign-in tokens, which keep you signed in for up to 7 days
  • Records of signed-out sessions, so an old sign-in cannot be reused
  • Temporary links for verifying your email and resetting your password

Your plan and usage

  • Your plan, purchased credits, generation counts and free allowance
  • Records of credit pack purchases

Payments

Stripe collects and processes your payment details. We do not receive or store your full card number. Stripe sends us payment details, such as your email address, payment status and card details limited to what Stripe provides, when it confirms a payment or when we prepare revenue totals. We do not save those details. We keep your Stripe customer ID, subscription ID and subscription status, along with payment event and checkout session IDs, so we can apply your plan and credits and avoid processing a payment twice. If you buy through a payment link, we use your checkout email address to match the purchase to your account.

The text you write

To generate a result, we process what you enter, such as an idea, a post, a customer review, an email or a job posting. On the Bundle plan in the extension, this can include your Brand Voice. Please think before including personal or sensitive information about yourself or anyone else.

Using X-Trend without an account

If you try the extension as a guest, we store a scrambled (keyed hash) version of your IP address and a daily count, so we can apply guest limits. We do not store your raw IP address for this purpose.

Technical information

Your IP address and basic request details pass through our servers and hosting and security providers, and may appear in their logs. To limit repeated attempts, we briefly hold your IP address, email address or Telegram ID in server memory. Our server logs record errors and billing events, and can include account or Stripe identifiers. They are not designed to record the text you write.

On your device

The extension keeps your sign-in token, email address, plan, Brand Voice, preferences and guest usage in Chrome's local storage on your device. Signing out removes your sign-in token, email address and plan details. Your Brand Voice, preferences and guest usage stay until you clear them or remove the extension.

Our website

Our website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. It remembers, for your current browser session, if you dismissed the announcement banner. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google, which means those companies receive your IP address when you visit. Google also handles your installation of the extension through the Chrome Web Store, under its own terms.

03

How We Use Your Information

  • To create your account, verify your email and sign you in
  • To generate the content you ask for
  • To apply your plan, credits and usage limits
  • To process payments, subscriptions and refunds, with Stripe
  • To send service emails, such as verification, password reset and Telegram connection emails
  • To keep X-Trend secure, and to prevent abuse and fix problems
  • To see overall totals for running the business, such as sign-ups, plans and revenue
  • To answer your questions and meet our legal obligations

We do not send marketing emails. Your plan and usage limits are applied automatically, and you can contact us if you think a limit has been applied incorrectly.

04

AI Writing Services

X-Trend uses Claude, an AI service from Anthropic. You are interacting with an AI system, not a human. When you generate content, the text you enter goes through our server to Anthropic, and the result comes back to you. We do not save that text or the result in our database, and we do not use it to train AI models.

Anthropic handles this information under its own terms, and may keep it for a limited period or longer in some cases, such as a safety review. See Anthropic's privacy information.

AI can make mistakes. Please check generated content before you use it.

05

X-Trend Telegram Bot

The Telegram bot is optional and works only in private chats. You must connect your X-Trend account before generating content. You must also meet Telegram's age requirements, as explained in section 11.

Connecting your account

Send /link with your X-Trend email address. If that email belongs to a verified account, we email a confirmation link. You then enter a code shown only in your Telegram chat. Link requests expire after 15 minutes, and we email you when the connection is made.

What we process

  • Your Telegram user ID, and your @username if you have one
  • The email address you send with /link
  • The link between your Telegram account and X-Trend account, and when it was made
  • A technical record of each Telegram update, so a message is not processed or charged twice
  • The commands you send, including any text you ask us to work on
  • Your IP address when you open the connection page, held briefly in server memory to limit repeated attempts

Telegram also sends us your display name and language with each message. We do not store them.

Generation requests

Your text is sent to Anthropic to produce a result, as described in section 4, and the result is sent back through Telegram. Your plan, credits and limits apply as they do in your account. Brand Voice is not used on Telegram. Telegram keeps your chat under its own terms, so X-Trend cannot delete messages from Telegram for you.

Disconnecting

Send /unlink and confirm. This removes the connection, and we email you to confirm. Resetting your password also stops the connection working until you connect again.

Automatic cleanup

Expired link requests are deleted automatically. Technical update records are deleted automatically after about 30 days. The 30-day cleanup does not apply to your connection, which stays until you remove it or your account is deleted.

06

Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share it with the providers listed below, and where the law requires or permits it.

  • Anthropic (United States): generates AI content from the text you send
  • Stripe (United States and other countries): processes payments and manages subscriptions
  • Supabase (database hosted in Singapore): stores account, plan, usage and Telegram records
  • Render (server hosted in the United States): runs the X-Trend server
  • Cloudflare (global network): carries and protects requests to our server
  • Resend (United States): delivers service emails, which can include your Telegram @username or user ID
  • Telegram (global network): delivers messages if you use the bot
  • GitHub and Google (United States and other countries): host our website, supply its fonts and, through Gmail, handle emails you send us
07

Overseas Disclosure

Your personal information is likely to be sent to, and stored by, recipients outside Australia. The countries we know of are the United States and Singapore. Stripe, Cloudflare, Telegram and other global providers may also handle it in other countries, depending on where their systems and your connection are located.

These recipients are subject to the laws of their own countries, which may differ from Australian privacy law.

08

How We Protect Your Information

We use encrypted connections, store passwords only as one-way hashes, restrict database access to our servers, account owner and database provider, and limit repeated sign-in and connection attempts. No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong password that you do not use anywhere else.

If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will take the steps the law requires, including notifying you where required.

09

How Long We Keep Information

  • Account, plan and usage information: while your account is open, and until it is deleted
  • Telegram connection: until you disconnect it or your account is deleted
  • Telegram link requests and update records: as described in section 5
  • Payment and purchase records: as long as we need them for tax, accounting and legal purposes. This includes checkout records, which can remain after an account is deleted. Stripe also keeps its own records
  • Signed-out session records: removed after they expire, the next time any user signs out
  • Guest usage: we do not yet delete this on a fixed schedule
  • Logs and backups: kept for the periods set by our providers, so a copy may remain for a time after deletion
10

Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and correction: email us to ask for a copy of your personal information or to correct it
  • Account deletion: email us from your account email address. There is no delete button in the extension yet. Deleting your account also removes your Telegram connection
  • Subscriptions: cancel at any time through the Stripe customer portal. Cancelling does not delete your account
  • Telegram: send /unlink at any time
  • Using X-Trend without identifying yourself: you can try the extension as a guest, but accounts, payments and the Telegram bot need your email address

We may need to confirm your identity first. We aim to respond within 30 days. If we cannot do what you ask, we will tell you why in writing and how to complain.

11

Children and Young People

You must be at least 13 years old to use X-Trend, including as a guest. To use the Telegram bot, you must also meet Telegram's age requirements. Telegram currently requires users in Australia, the United Kingdom and the European Union to be at least 18.

X-Trend is not directed to children under 13. We do not ask for or check your age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

If you are under 18

  • X-Trend is an AI tool, not a person. It can get things wrong, so check what it writes
  • Keep personal details out of what you type, both yours and other people's
  • Ask a parent or guardian before buying a subscription or credit pack
  • If something worries or upsets you, stop and talk to an adult you trust. You can also contact us or get help from the eSafety Commissioner

We aim to comply with the child safety and privacy laws that apply to X-Trend, including, where it applies, the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act.
